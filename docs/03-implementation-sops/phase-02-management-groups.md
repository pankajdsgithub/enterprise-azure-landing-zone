# Phase 02: Management Groups

## Objective
Implement the management group hierarchy that supports governance and inheritance.

## Manual Steps
1. align the hierarchy with the expected platform model
2. create management groups for platform, landing zones, sandbox, and decommissioned scopes
3. verify parent-child configuration and inheritance
4. confirm that policy and RBAC will apply correctly at the desired hierarchy level

## Repository Mapping
This phase maps to the management group module and governance roots in the repo.

## Validation
- confirm governance hierarchy existence in Azure Portal
- validate that policy inheritance is active at the landing zone and platform scopes
- confirm naming conventions match the enterprise standard

## Exit Criteria
The management group model is ready for governance enforcement and policy assignment.
