# Phase 04: Governance and Policy

## Objective
Apply the governance guardrails that enforce naming, tagging, location, and standard configuration policies.

## Manual Steps
1. review the custom policy definitions in the repository
2. confirm the policy set aligns with business requirements
3. assign policy definitions at management group scope for inheritance
4. review policy effect and exemptions before applying

## Repository Mapping
This phase aligns with the policy and policy initiative modules plus the governance root.

## Validation
- confirm policy assignment works at the correct hierarchy node
- validate that tags required by the enterprise are being enforced or audited
- review whether exceptions are required for dev or sandbox environments

## Exit Criteria
The governance baseline is active and aligned with enterprise control expectations.
