# Operations and Support Model

## Purpose
Define who owns the platform, how support is structured, and how operational issues are escalated.

## Support Model
- platform engineering owns the control plane and landing zone baseline
- application teams own their workload-specific resources and deployment logic
- security and governance teams validate policy and control exceptions
- operations validates monitoring, incident, and response procedures

## Service Expectations
- health checks and alerting are configured
- incident ownership is assigned
- support runbooks are maintained
- a handover process exists for platform changes and troubleshooting

## Outcome
The landing zone is not merely deployed; it is supportable, auditable, and governed in operational practice.
