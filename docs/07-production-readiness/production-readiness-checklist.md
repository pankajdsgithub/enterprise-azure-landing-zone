# Production Readiness Checklist

## Objective
Before a landing zone is treated as production-ready, the project must meet a formal checklist covering governance, support, security, and operational stability.

## Checklist
- management group and subscription boundaries validated
- monitoring and diagnostics in place
- security baselines reviewed and approved
- policy and RBAC confirmed as least privilege
- remote state security reviewed
- environment-specific approvals recorded
- support ownership assigned

## Review Gate
Production readiness requires sign-off from architecture, security, and operations owners.

## Outcome
The environment is documented, supported, and ready for controlled business use without ad hoc operational risk.
