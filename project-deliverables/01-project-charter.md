# Project Charter

## 1. Project Information
- Project Title: Integrated Citizen Service Delivery Platform
- Department: Government Department
- Strategic Objective: Improve service delivery, transparency, and accountability using digital workflows
- Project Type: Government Digital Transformation

## 2. Background
The department currently processes citizen applications using paper-based workflows and fragmented procedures. This creates delays, procedural duplication, inconsistent decisions, and poor visibility across offices. The absence of a centralized view hampers performance measurement and creates dissatisfaction among citizens.

## 3. Problem Statement
Citizen applications are processed manually through multiple desks and approval levels. Files move across sections, data is not centralized, and monitoring is difficult. These issues result in longer turnaround times, service bottlenecks, weak accountability, and an inconsistent citizen experience.

## 4. Project Goal
To implement a digital citizen service platform that supports automated workflow handling, application tracking, reporting, dashboard-based monitoring, and efficient service delivery.

## 5. Objectives
- Reduce processing time for citizen applications
- Improve public trust and service satisfaction
- Standardize operational processes across offices
- Improve transparency and governance
- Enable MIS reporting and performance dashboards
- Minimize paperwork and reduce human error

## 6. Scope
### In Scope
- Online citizen application intake
- Unique application ID tracking
- Workflow-based approvals and escalations
- Document upload and verification
- Citizen status tracking
- Officer dashboard and admin oversight
- Reporting, MIS, and KPI monitoring
- Notifications and alerts

### Out of Scope
- Non-service functions unrelated to application processing
- Unrelated departmental reforms outside the approved digital process
- Complete restructuring of all legacy systems across all departments

## 7. Stakeholders
- Department Head
- Joint Secretary / Nodal Officer
- IT Cell
- Finance and Administration
- District Offices
- Citizens and applicants
- Project Management Consultant
- Vendor / System Integrator

## 8. Assumptions
- Existing service processes can be mapped and digitized
- Department will provide required data and user inputs
- Necessary network and hosting resources will be available
- Staff will participate in onboarding and training

## 9. Constraints
- Budget limitations
- Procurement compliance rules
- Existing infrastructure constraints
- Need for data migration from legacy systems
- Operational continuity during transition

## 10. Governance Structure
### Steering Committee
Responsible for overall direction, approvals, and strategic issue resolution.

### Project Management Unit
Responsible for day-to-day monitoring, coordination, schedule tracking, and management reporting.

### Technical Review Group
Responsible for architecture review, security checks, design sign-off, and technical approvals.

## 11. Success Criteria
- Reduced average processing time
- Increased application transparency
- Improved stakeholder satisfaction
- Accurate dashboards and reporting
- Stable deployment with minimal defect backlog

## 12. Approvals
Prepared by: Project Management Team
Reviewed by: Department Nodal Officer and IT Cell
Approved by: Steering Committee
