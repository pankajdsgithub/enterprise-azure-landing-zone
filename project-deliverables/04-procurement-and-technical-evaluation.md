# Procurement and Technical Evaluation

## 1. RFP and Bid Documents

### Invitation to Bid
The department invites proposals from qualified firms for the design, development, implementation, training, and support of the Integrated Citizen Service Delivery Platform.

### Scope of Work
- Process review and requirements validation
- Solution design and configuration
- Platform development and integration
- Workflow and dashboard configuration
- Testing, UAT, and issue resolution
- Training and support

### Eligibility Criteria
- Relevant experience in digital platform delivery
- Previous public-sector or government project experience
- Ability to provide project team and technical experts
- Proven portfolio of workflow-based systems

### Submission Requirements
- Technical proposal
- Commercial proposal
- Company profile and references
- Team structure and CVs
- Implementation plan

### Evaluation Criteria
- Technical capability — 40%
- Functional fit — 25%
- Implementation approach — 15%
- Past performance — 10%
- Cost — 10%

---

## 2. Technical Evaluation Report

### Summary
Three bids were received. The proposals were assessed on technical soundness, implementation feasibility, and overall value.

| Bidder | Technical Score | Financial Score | Overall Score | Status |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Vendor A | 86 | 74 | 80 | Recommended |
| Vendor B | 78 | 82 | 80 | Shortlisted |
| Vendor C | 68 | 88 | 78 | Not recommended |

### Findings
- Vendor A demonstrated strong workflow capability and good project understanding.
- Vendor A offered better integration and audit capability.
- Vendor B had a lower cost but weaker implementation clarity.
- Vendor C lacked strong evidence in public-sector service platforms.

### Recommendation
Vendor A is recommended for award subject to approval by the competent authority.

### Decision
Procurement is approved and vendor mobilization begins.
