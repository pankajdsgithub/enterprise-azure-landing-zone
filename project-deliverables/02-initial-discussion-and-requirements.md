# Initial Discussion Note and Requirement Capture

## 1. Initial Departmental Discussion Note

### Meeting details
- Date: 04 January 2026
- Department: Government Department
- Purpose: Discussion on delays in citizen service processing and need for digital intervention

### Background
The department processes a large volume of citizen applications involving verification, approval, and service issuance. Many activities continue through manual paperwork and individual desk-level action. This leads to inconsistent handling and delays.

### Key observations
- Average processing time is high
- Files move physically between sections
- Citizens have poor visibility of case status
- Data is fragmented and not centrally managed
- Officers rely on manual follow-up methods

### Discussion outcome
The department agreed that a digital solution is required to streamline operations and strengthen public service delivery. The proposed platform should support online submission, case tracking, document verification, and dashboard reporting.

### Decision
The department approved proceeding to requirement capture and concept preparation.

---

## 2. Requirement Capture Note

### Objective
To document business, functional, technical, and operational requirements for the digital platform.

### Business Requirements
- Improve turnaround time
- Increase transparency to citizens and department staff
- Standardize workflow rules and approval structure
- Provide better monitoring and reporting

### Functional Requirements
- Citizen registration and online application submission
- Case creation with unique ID
- Role-based access for citizen, clerk, officer, administrator
- Workflow routing and escalations
- Online document upload and verification
- SMS/email notification to citizens
- Dashboards for pending, approved, rejected, and delayed cases
- Reporting module and MIS export capability

### Non-Functional Requirements
- 99.5% availability
- Secure data storage
- Audit trail for actions
- Mobile-friendly user interface
- Multi-user capability
- Data backup and recovery

### Constraints
- Existing legacy systems may need integration
- Department staff need training on new workflows
- Budget and procurement norms must be followed

### Proposed Scope
- Citizen portal
- Application workflow engine
- Reporting and monitoring module
- Admin and audit functions
- Integration layer

### Outcome
The requirement note is approved and used as the baseline for concept note and DPR preparation.

---

## 3. Concept Note

### Project Rationale
The current service delivery process is time-consuming and lacks traceability. A digital platform can create a single, transparent, and accountable service model.

### Proposed Solution
The project will introduce an Integrated Citizen Service Delivery Platform with case workflow management, citizen portal, digital document support, dashboard reporting, and approval tracking.

### Alternatives Considered
- Continue with manual workflow
- Improve spreadsheets and file tracking
- Implement a new digital service platform

### Recommendation
A new digital platform is recommended because it provides better governance, scalability, and public service improvement.

### Expected Benefits
- Simplified service delivery
- Reduced manual workload
- Faster approvals and reduced delays
- Better management visibility
- More citizen-centric experience
